# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.
- Accept feedback professionally.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other reasonable moderation action.

Report concerns privately through the security contact in `SECURITY.md`.
